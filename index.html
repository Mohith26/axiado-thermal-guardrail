Thermal management

The DTM agent sets fan duty from its 30-second temperature forecast. Thermal guardrail checks every fan cut against a calibrated upper bound on that forecast and vetoes cuts that could push a sensor past its upper critical threshold.

Calibration

Bounds are calibrated separately for each distance-to-limit band, from yesterday's forecast errors, and kept honest online: every miss tightens that band's bound (adaptive conformal inference over the most recent 2 hours of scores).
